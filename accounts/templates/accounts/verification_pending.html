01

Why verification matters

Verification helps Smart City Uganda confirm that accounts belong to the appropriate people. It supports safer access to public services, responsible handling of requests, and reliable communication between citizens and authorised staff.

Account protection

Helps reduce impersonation and unauthorised access.

Identity confirmation

Allows the verification team to review your submitted information.

Appropriate service access

Supports access according to your account role and permissions.

02

Verification rules

Read these guidelines before submitting your verification details.

Provide accurate information

Use your correct personal or authorised staff details.

Submit genuine documents

Documents must be authentic, readable, and belong to you or be lawfully authorised for your use.

Protect your private information

Upload documents only through the official verification form. Never share passwords or one-time security codes.

Wait for the review outcome

Submitting your details does not automatically mean your account has been approved.

Correct any issues

If your submission is rejected, read the feedback and provide corrected information where permitted.

Important privacy notice

Only submit information requested by the verification form. Access to identity documents should be restricted to authorised reviewers. Do not upload unrelated sensitive information.
